# Brief compliance audit (Session L, policy v1)

Generated by `python -m step1_data_generation.brief_audit` from `data/<split>/cases.jsonl` + `truth.jsonl`; rerun it after any rebuild. Counts are **cases** that show the detail (a percentage of the split), except where a row says hits. **THIN** = under 3% of train cases; **MISSING** = no train case. Examples are train case ids.

Splits: `train` (3,000 base cases), `train_twins` (1,000 counterfactual twins of train cases), `train_knockout` (300 evidence knockouts of train cases), `dev` (150), `eval_id` (300), `eval_ood_template` (50, the template part of the OOD set; the 100 LLM-rendered OOD packets and the 50 hand templates are built by Session M). The eval sets are built but **not frozen** yet (the freeze follows the dev pilot).
